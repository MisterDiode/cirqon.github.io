Engineering priorities

Engineering development requires rigour and reliability.

Effective electronics development requires sound technical decisions, documented processes, and consistent attention to performance, reliability, and manufacturability.

Design quality and schedule

Development schedules must account for design reviews, verification activities, and engineering decisions that affect long-term product reliability.

Resource and cost management

Embedded development requires specialized expertise, appropriate planning, and efficient allocation of engineering resources.

Application-specific design

Reference designs require evaluation against system requirements, operating conditions, and manufacturing constraints before adoption.

Technical accountability

Clear documentation, transparent technical decisions, and regular design reviews support effective collaboration throughout development.
